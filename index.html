Meet the Founder
SN

Hi, I'm Sthabile Nzama, the founder of Help Me Help You.

I'm a 21-year-old web developer, Licensed Esport player, bead artisan, as well as a Biology student. I come from a background that has taught me the importance of determination, creativity and creating opportunities for yourself.

I may not have come from a privileged background, but I have always believed that your starting point does not have to determine where you end up. Throughout my journey, I have explored different skills and interests—from science and technology to esports and entrepreneurship.

I created Help Me Help You because I understand how challenging it can be for students and young people to find opportunities. Sometimes people have the skills and potential but struggle to present themselves professionally or simply don't know where to look.

My goal is to make that process easier.

Through this platform, I help students and job seekers create professional CVs and stay informed about opportunities that may match their interests and career goals.

I'm building this platform one step at a time, with one simple belief:

"Help Me Help You"

When we support each other and share opportunities, we can all move closer to where we want to be.
